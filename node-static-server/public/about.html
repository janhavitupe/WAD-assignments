About EduAdmin India

Empowering institutions across India with smart administration tools.

Our Mission

EduAdmin India aims to digitise and simplify college administration — from admissions and fee collection to exam scheduling and result publication — for institutions across every state in India.

Our Team

We are a team of engineers, educators and designers based in Pune, Maharashtra, committed to building accessible and affordable EdTech solutions for Tier 2 and Tier 3 colleges.

Technology Stack

Built with Node.js, Angular, and Bootstrap — deployed on Docker containers for reliability and scalability across Indian data centres.
